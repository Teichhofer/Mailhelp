Privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

Data controller

Hartmann GbR, Stefan Hartmann, Teichhof 1, 74670 Sindringen, Germany. Contact inboxstride@email.de.

Expressions of interest

If you register your interest using our form, we store your email address, the date and time, your chosen language, and the text and version of your consent. We use this information to contact you by email about the launch of Inboxstride. Processing is based on your consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR.

Consent is voluntary. You can withdraw it at any time with effect for the future by emailing inboxstride@email.de. This does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing. We delete your interest record following withdrawal. Records are automatically deleted after a maximum of twelve months.

Website operation

The website and interest records are stored on the server infrastructure managed by the operator. Technical hosting providers may receive data when providing that infrastructure. Connection information such as your IP address and the requested file is processed as needed to deliver this website and limit abuse. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, particularly the secure and reliable operation of the website. Regular access logging is disabled for this landing page. Form contents are not written to error logs.

Local content and video

Fonts, images and the embedded video are served from our own server. The player loads the video for playback; no external video provider is embedded. The video is an AI-generated illustration, not a recording of the application. The interest form sends your enquiry to our own server.

Your cookie choice

Google services load only after you voluntarily choose “Allow analytics”. “Necessary only” declines analytics; the website and form remain available. Without a choice or without JavaScript, no Google analytics runs, including no consent-free measurement requests. Use “Cookie settings” in the footer to change your choice or withdraw consent at any time for the future. Withdrawal reloads the page and removes Google Analytics cookies accessible to this website. Earlier lawful processing is unaffected.

The necessary inboxstride_consent cookie stores only your choice, its time and the notice version for up to 180 days, without a visitor identifier. This remembers and enforces your decision. Its necessary storage is based on section 25(2)(2) TDDDG; associated processing is based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. We ask again after expiry or a relevant change.

Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics

With consent, we use Google Tag Manager (container GTM-55C4ZT5N) to load Google Analytics 4 (ID G-ZQP60GV6NS). The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. The purpose is statistical analysis of this website, including page views, use and technical performance. Processing may include cookie identifiers, visited pages, interactions, timestamps, referring pages, browser/device details and the IP address needed for transmission. Google states that Analytics 4 does not store individual IP addresses. Processing is not anonymous: cookie identifiers allow recognition of returning visitors.

The legal bases are your consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and section 25(1) TDDDG. Analytics consent is separate from consent to launch contact. Advertising storage, ad personalization and permission to provide user data to Google remain denied in consent mode. Our integration does not send the form email address to analytics and strips query parameters and URL fragments from page and referrer addresses it sets.

Google Analytics may use cookies including _ga and _ga_…. Google gives a default lifetime of up to two years; our integration requests a maximum of 180 days without ongoing renewal. Browsers may shorten these periods. Event- and user-level data deletion at Google also depends on the Analytics property's retention settings; standard properties offer two or 14 months. Aggregated statistics may be retained longer. The specific property setting has not yet been verified; contact us for further information.

Recipients may include Google Ireland, Google LLC and service providers used by Google. Processing in the United States is possible. Google describes transfer mechanisms including the EU-US Data Privacy Framework for certified recipients and standard contractual clauses. Further information is available in Google's international transfer framework, Google's privacy policy and how Google uses data from partner services.

Live demo at demo.inboxstride.de

The separate demo uses 30 synthetic sample emails. It does not connect a real inbox, Telegram, AI service, calendar or task account. Decisions only change your own demo workspace. Please do not enter real personal or confidential information in demo fields.

For secure use, the demo sets necessary host-only cookies: mh_pair for up to 15 minutes to protect starting the demo and mh_session for the two-hour session. mailhelp_lang remembers your chosen language for up to one year. Session data, demo decisions and any text you enter are stored on our server separately from other visitors. Expired visitor workspaces are regularly deleted while the service is running. For abuse prevention, the client IP address is temporarily processed in memory to limit starts. Regular nginx access logging is disabled for the demo; technical operating events are logged.

Necessary cookies are based on section 25(2)(2) TDDDG. Related processing to provide the demo you request and operate it securely is based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Google Analytics is not embedded in the demo. Following the link from the landing page does not connect an inbox or send your form email address to the demo.

Inboxstride and connected accounts

Inboxstride is the hosted offering based on Mailhelp software. The following information concerns enabled accounts using real data; the public demo uses synthetic examples only. The general hosted service is still in preparation. An expression of interest does not connect a mailbox or grant Google access. Available features and the settings applicable to an account are provided when access is enabled.

Hartmann GbR, identified above, is the controller for contact, contract, access and operational data. Where business customers entrust personal content to us for processing under their instructions, the respective roles and any required data processing agreement are established before use. Connecting an account does not replace your responsibility to ensure that you are entitled to process the data it contains.

Application data we process

  • Mailbox data: sender, recipients, subject, date, message identifiers, message text and supported attachments. Regular IMAP retrieval does not modify messages or their read status. Optional IServ parent letters and attachments are processed as incoming messages.
  • AI results: relevance assessments, summaries, task and event proposals, source evidence, clarification questions and your decisions. Optional functions include reply drafts, expressly initiated activity reports and learning functions.
  • Connected calendars and task lists: required calendar/list identifiers and entries used for matching and verifying confirmed actions. An enabled daily overview uses the calendar and task information it needs.
  • Access and security: account settings, OAuth credentials, session identifiers in the form needed for authentication, device labels, pairing times, technical status/error data and records preventing duplicate actions.
  • Optional interaction channels: Telegram inputs, selected notification content, browser push subscriptions and help-chat questions. Browser push notifications contain no email or calendar content.

We process this data to provide your selected features, connect accounts, associate proposals and execute confirmed actions securely. Where we act as controller, necessary pre-contractual and contractual processing is based on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Security, troubleshooting and abuse prevention rely on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is reliable and secure operation. Separately offered voluntary processing is based on the applicable consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR. Legal obligations rely on Article 6(1)(c) GDPR. OAuth consent technically authorises access; it does not by itself provide a legal basis for all content in an account.

Google Calendar data and permissions

A Google connection is established through OAuth only when you initiate it. We use calendar.events to read, create, update and delete events, calendar.calendars.readonly to check the selected calendar, and calendar.calendarlist.readonly for calendar selection. Each full permission name begins with https://www.googleapis.com/auth/. These calendar permissions provide no access to Gmail or Google Drive.

Read data includes the title, description, location, times and available conference information of matching events. Calendar data helps identify the same event, avoid duplicates, associate confirmed updates and verify results. Creating, changing, cancelling or removing an entry requires explicit approval of the specific proposal version in the authenticated web interface or Telegram. Editing, saving, connecting an account or signing in again does not itself authorise such a write.

For semantic calendar matching, the title, description, location and times of relevant existing events may be sent to the configured AI service together with a proposal. If enabled, a daily overview may display calendar information in the application and in Telegram according to your selected content profile. Calendar access is separate from consent to public-website analytics; our application does not send email, calendar, task or OAuth content to Google Analytics.

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only for the described user-facing features, is not sold, is not used for advertising, and is not used or shared to develop or train general-purpose AI models. Human access is limited to your explicit consent or the security, legal and narrowly defined operational cases permitted by that policy.

You may revoke access at any time in your Google Account connections. Inboxstride cannot subsequently access that calendar without renewed authorisation. Revocation does not automatically delete existing calendar entries or previously lawfully stored data; request deletion of data held by us using our contact address.

AI processing and other recipients

Depending on the enabled configuration, email and attachment content, proposal fields, clarification answers and calendar details needed for matching are sent to OpenRouter and the selected model providers. The help chat receives your question, a short conversation history and relevant documentation excerpts. Expressly initiated learning runs may use the selected messages and corrections. The providers and models in use can be identified from the account configuration or by contacting us. AI processing may include personal information about you, senders and other people mentioned in content.

Other recipients include your connected email/IServ providers and, where enabled, Google Calendar, Todoist, Microsoft services or CalDAV providers, each receiving data necessary for its function. Telegram receives messages according to your selected content profile; direct proposals and questions may include email excerpts. Browser push services receive the necessary subscription endpoint and an encrypted notification without content. Hosting is provided by centron in Germany, which supplies the server infrastructure. Credentials are not passed to the AI model.

External services may process data outside the EU/EEA. The necessary transfer basis must apply to each recipient, such as an applicable adequacy decision or appropriate safeguards including standard contractual clauses. The recipients and safeguards applicable to your account are made available before access is enabled or through our contact address. The website analytics information above applies separately to public-website use.

Storage, retention and deletion of application data

Application data is stored on infrastructure managed by the operator and hosted by centron in Germany, rather than exclusively on your device. AI processing uses OpenRouter and the selected model providers; this does not mean that all processing is limited to Germany. Credentials and refresh tokens are kept outside the publicly served website directory. Account-specific retention rules are provided when access is enabled and can be checked in the application or requested from us.

The currently operated Mailhelp configuration retains full email text for 30 days after a process is completed; decision history defaults to 90 days. AI intermediate results are removed during the next cleanup under the current configuration. Open processes remain protected until completion. Learning records of manual decisions may remain until deleted by the authorised person. Proposals, write receipts and compact references are retained as necessary for functionality, traceability and duplicate prevention.

Technical application logs currently have 30-day retention with additional size rotation. Full AI requests/responses and Telegram content may appear in separately and explicitly enabled diagnostic logs; the currently operated instance has such content logging enabled, also with 30-day retention and size rotation. Rotation may remove data sooner. These notices must not be understood as a promise that no content is logged. Newly enabled accounts receive information about their actual settings.

To request deletion or end a connection, contact inboxstride@email.de. After the contractual relationship ends, we delete customer data within 14 days unless statutory retention obligations apply. This deadline also limits otherwise longer internal application retention periods for personal customer data. Data subject to statutory retention is restricted to what is necessary, retained only for the relevant statutory purpose and deleted when the obligation ends. Deletion by us does not remove messages from the original mailbox or entries already created with calendar/task providers. Deletion after the contract ends must also be accounted for in backup management. Data held independently by connected third-party providers is additionally subject to their rules and the deletion options available to you there.

Security and your control

Web traffic is encrypted using HTTPS. Application sessions are access-protected; web write actions require a valid session, CSRF protection, version checks and an idempotency key. Email content and AI outputs are treated as untrusted. The installable application's offline cache stores its shell, not your emails or proposals. Necessary session/security cookies support the login and interaction you request (section 25(2)(2) TDDDG).

AI results may contain errors and need review before approval. There is no solely automated decision producing legal or similarly significant effects within the meaning of Article 22 GDPR. The rights described below also apply to application data and connected accounts.

Your rights

Subject to the applicable legal conditions, you have rights of access, correction, erasure, restriction, data portability and withdrawal of consent. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests on grounds relating to your particular situation. You also have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority, including the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information Baden-Württemberg.

To exercise your rights, contact inboxstride@email.de.
