A closer look at how Inboxstride can help with your day.

What can Inboxstride do with my emails?

Long messages become clear summaries. Requests and deadlines become task suggestions. Invitations and changes become prepared calendar entries. See what matters and decide on your next step.

Which inboxes, calendars and task lists are planned?

Development supports IMAP inboxes and connections for Google Calendar, Outlook and CalDAV. Task connections include Todoist, Microsoft To Do, CalDAV and Apple Reminders. We will announce which connections the hosted service offers at launch, along with their requirements.

Can I use it on my phone?

The interface is designed for mobile and desktop browsers. Review and approve suggestions on the go. A mobile view is part of the planned service.

How will my email data be processed?

The hosted service is still being prepared. Specific details about hosting, AI providers, permissions and retention are pending and will be provided before you connect an inbox. This signup page does not read your emails: you only leave your contact address here.

Will I need technical knowledge?

Inboxstride is being developed as a hosted service. Use it in your browser and focus on your emails and decisions. We take care of the technical operation.

Do I stay in control of my tasks and calendar?

Yes. You review the prepared suggestions and approve what is added to your calendar or task list. The decision stays with you.

Can I try Inboxstride already?

Yes. The linked live demo lets you explore the interface with 30 prepared sample emails. No account or service connection is needed. Actions are simulated in your own workspace, which lasts two hours. Start the demo in English or German. The hosted service for your own inbox is still being prepared.
