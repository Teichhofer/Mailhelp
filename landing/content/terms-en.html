3. What Inboxstride does

Inboxstride reads authorised connected mailboxes, uses AI to analyse content and supported attachments, and prepares tasks, deadlines and calendar events for review. Optional available features may include IServ parent letters, daily overviews, reply drafts, activity reports and calendar/task matching. The scope agreed when access is enabled applies.

AI results can be inaccurate, incomplete or outdated. Check dates, times, locations, meeting access details, responsibility and deadlines against the original source. Inboxstride does not replace reading important messages or maintaining your own deadline controls.
